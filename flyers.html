Founder's 30-Day Campus Guerrilla Marketing Strategy

The battle-tested playbook for scaling Campally from 0 to 5,000+ active student users across faculties and hostels.

Week 1: Ground Zero Blitz

Faculty Noticeboards & Hostel Porters

Print 50 A4/A5 flyers. Partner with hostel porters and student hall executives. Pin flyers directly beside the porter's desk and main library entrance where student footfall is highest.

Week 2: Course Rep Alliance

The Departmental Drop

Meet with 10 key course reps (100L to 400L). Provide them with customized flyers featuring their unique Ambassador code. When course reps drop the past questions alert in class WhatsApp groups, conversions skyrocket.

Week 3: Ambassador Bounty Contest

Campus Referral Sprint

Host a 7-day "Top Campus Ambassador" challenge. The ambassador with the most verified listings and signups wins ₦25,000 cash bonus paid straight to their bank account via Paystack transfer.

Week 4: Peak Momentum Timing

Exam Rush & Hostel Clear-Out

Target exam weeks and semester endings. Run the "Clear Your Hostel" and "70% Off Handouts" flyers. Students urgently wanting to buy past questions or sell heavy textbooks will flood the platform.

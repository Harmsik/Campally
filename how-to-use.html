1. Getting Started & Selecting Your Campus

Campally connects students within their university communities across Nigeria. Whether you are a registered student or browsing as a visitor:

  • 1 Type Your Campus: Use the live campus search at the top of the homepage (e.g. FUNAI, UNILAG, UNN, Ibadan, OAU). Campally filters all listings specifically to your school.
  • 2 Filter by Hostel or Lodge: Use the Hostel dropdown to see items available right in your lodge or perm site hall, making meetups effortless.
  • 3 Visitor Mode: Anyone can browse deals as a visitor. When you're ready to sell, post requests, or verify your student status, simply sign in or create an account.

2. How to Buy Textbooks, Notes & Essentials

Finding deals and contacting other students is 100% free with zero agent commissions:

  • 1 Search Course Codes: Need past questions or textbooks for a specific course? Type course codes like MTH101, GST102, or PHY111 directly into search.
  • 2 Category Chips & Budget Pills: One-tap filter by Academic, Electronics, Hostel Essentials, Fashion, or Budget (e.g. Under ₦2,000, Under ₦5,000).
  • 3 Direct WhatsApp Chat: Tap "Chat with Seller on WhatsApp" on any listing to negotiate directly with the student seller.
  • 4 Save Favorites: Tap the heart icon on any product to save it to your "Saved Items" list for quick review later.

3. Campus Safety & Zero-Scam Rules

Campally is built on student trust. Follow these 5 Golden Rules for every transaction:

CRITICAL RULE: NEVER SEND MONEY IN ADVANCE! Never transfer money via bank or mobile money before physically seeing, inspecting, and testing the item in person. Any seller demanding "delivery fee" or advance deposit before meetup is likely a scammer.
  • 1 Meet in Public Campus Spots: Always choose safe daytime meetup spots:
    • University Main Library Foyer
    • Student Union Building (SUB) Entrance
    • Hostel Porter's Lodge or Reception Desk
    • Faculty Lecture Theater Corridors
  • 2 Inspect Thoroughly: Test calculators, laptops, chargers, and appliances on the spot before handing over cash or completing transfer.
  • 3 Bring a Friend: Especially for electronics or higher-value transactions, walk with a roommate or classmate.
  • 4 Report Suspicious Activity: If anyone behaves fraudulently, email us immediately at mbuharmless@gmail.com so we can ban the account.

4. How to Sell or Swap Items Fast

Turn your old textbooks, room accessories, or gadgets into quick cash:

  • 1 Post with Real Photos: Good daylight lighting makes items sell 3x faster.
  • 2 Specify Your Hostel & Course: Helps students in your hall or department find you immediately.
  • 3 Open to Swap / Trade: Check the "Open to Swap" checkbox if you're open to trading your item for something else.
  • 4 Manage Your Inventory: In "My Listings", you can mark items as Sold or relist them with a single click.

About Campally

Campally is a marketplace built by students, for students. Buy and sell textbooks, past questions, electronics, fashion, and services — all within your own campus community. No strangers, no long-distance shipping — just students helping students, one listing at a time.

Our Vision

We believe every student has something valuable to offer — a skill, an item, a service — and every student has a need someone nearby can meet. Campally exists to close that gap, making campus life a little easier, a little more connected, and a little more affordable, one trade at a time.

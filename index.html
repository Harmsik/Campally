About Campally

Launched initially at Alex Ekwueme Federal University Ndufu-Alike (FUNAI), Campally is now expanding to serve students across all universities and polytechnics in Nigeria. Buy and sell textbooks, past questions, electronics, fashion, and services — within your own campus community. No strangers, no high delivery costs — just verified students helping students, one listing at a time.

Our Vision

We believe every student has something valuable to offer — a skill, an item, a service — and every student has a need someone nearby can meet. Campally connects campus communities nationwide to make student life affordable, seamless, and safe.
